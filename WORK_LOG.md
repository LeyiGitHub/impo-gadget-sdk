The servo bus was the hard part: replies lose a byte or a bit while the motors run (at rest 40 of 40 reads came back whole at 0.97–1.03 Mbit/s), and after a move against a stop (0.6–0.7 A on the body battery) a servo could stay silent across ESP32 resets until its power was switched off. - 2026-11-25, later: Voice notes go up as AAC on boards with PSRAM (`impo_note_aac.c` on Espressif's esp_audio_codec 2.6.2: AAC-LC, 16 kHz mono, 24 kbit/s, ADTS, as the phone apps record theirs) instead of a base64 16 kHz WAV.
